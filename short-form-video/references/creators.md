## Working the list

Take one account and five to ten of its recent videos, then transcribe the first sentence of each. The hook pattern shows up faster in a column of ten opening lines than in ten viewings, and it transfers directly into the hook step.

Beyond the hook, the transferable parts are the ones the pharmacy can copy without the following: how long the presenter waits before naming themselves, where the story sits relative to the ask, whether the close hands over criteria or jumps to the product, and how much of the frame the face occupies. Follower counts are not one of these — a video that works at two million followers is often carried by the name in the corner, which the pharmacy does not have yet.
